## What we changed

The meshes are used as geometry only. Each one is rescaled and repositioned to fit the
simulation domain and converted into a solid voxel mask; no shape, texture, or material
was altered or redistributed in modified form.

Full provenance, including download URLs and per-mesh vertex/face counts, is recorded in
`assets/meshes/LICENSES.md`.
